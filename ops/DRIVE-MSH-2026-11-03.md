Release source: `e1810e3` (1.26.0), followed by `0a8f3b4` (1.26.1 content-sizing correction). This is a scoped Drive/Research repair, not a claim that all 27 rooms passed a new visual audit.

## Task and hierarchy

Choose a road and a car, watch what its camera sees, then understand why it brakes. The moving windshield wins, its decision comes second, road/vehicle choices and the map give context. Changes to traffic and signal policy form one nearby experiment. Research leads with the actual sticker film and a comparison of the teaching methods, followed by working examples.

## Movement and scientific correctness

- Continuous position/heading interpolation removes half-metre jumps at low speed. Lane search preserves those same interpolated positions without allocating full poses.
- Footway movement uses bounded time-based displacement, including a consistent initial pose. The old update integrated absolute time and accelerated after long sessions.
- Driver-only mobile visibility now advances physics. Changing roads/policies with a hidden map no longer tries to draw a zero-sized canvas.
- Shared-coordinate vehicle profiles, roofs/windows/tyres/lights, clipped faces and mesh-aligned detection boxes replace the former flat vehicle rectangles. Objects paint by depth. Lamp arms and signs are drawn at their mounting heights. Road paint is fixed to road coordinates; paint/footways are cut at Asok conflict zones instead of crisscrossing the junction. This remains a lightweight painter renderer, not a calibrated3D sensor simulator.
- Fixed9-second greens compare with a queue-responsive9–18-second rule. Both preserve2.5s amber/1.5s all-red and alternate sides. Controller queue input is ideal simulated state, explicitly not real CCTV.
- Clear-exit detection uses the car's own tracks and the end of Asok's conflict zone, rather than a fixed offset inside its enlarged junction. Crashed vehicles remain blockers. Stopped vehicle-time includes crash downtime. The dashboard and rendered frame update together; unknown desired gap prints a dash.
- Eight focused behavioral tests cover continuous/wrapped motion, long-session/initial walker motion, controller clearance/fairness, blocked exits and blind cameras, source geometry, near-plane clipping, crash time and Asok's exit anchor. Existing long-run driving tests remain green; an optimization that failed the daylight harm assertion was corrected rather than weakening that assertion.

## Geographic evidence and limits

The 200×140m study is centred on13.73642,100.56128. OSM carriageway polylines were retrieved and simplified into locally shipped routes.
