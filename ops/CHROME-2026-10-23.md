Task: choose a lesson, act on a picture, and read the result without losing your place. Dominant element: the picture being taught; next priorities are the teaching action and its immediate guidance.

## Found

The global room rail and four-step learning path repeated navigation in separate bands. On the measured English release, they consumed 145px (17.2%) of a 390×844 screen and 101px (11.2%) at 1024×900 and 1440×900. The full instruction lists preceded the first teaching action. That action started at y=1233 on the phone, 928 on the tablet, and 898 on desktop, separating the picture from its action.

The comparison reported into the earlier lesson's status line. An actual Ask on public 1.22.0 moved its button from document y=1341.1875 to1397.1875: 56px. The layout expansion was upstream of the button being pressed.

## Changed

- One content-sized44px header. On phones, Rooms opens a named menu; desktop keeps primary rooms directly visible. All rooms remain accessible. The four-step path, including its current step and descriptions, lives inside More on the four relevant rooms. Menu height is requested by the user, not reserved at load.
- Native More disclosure; Escape dismisses More then Rooms, returning focus to the respective opener. Clicking outside dismisses navigation. If scripts fail, primary room links remain visible as a fallback rather than being hidden behind a dead button.
- Teach's title and explanation stay in one reading group. Removed the redundant room-number eyebrow. Full instructions remain in native disclosures. The supervised teaching buttons precede the guide, so the short live guidance and action can be read together.
- Scene choices fit their contents (195px English, 240px Thai), rather than stretching across a500px control panel.
- Three method descriptions wrap within their columns rather than running into a tablet scroller.
- Comparison progress/results have a local live status below Ask. Reading/loading/error messages use that reporter too, so they do not expand an earlier lesson region. No empty spacer or reserved banner was added.
